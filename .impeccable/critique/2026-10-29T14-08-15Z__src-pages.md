## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Moderately high on the public homepage, uneven across the site. The red-and-warm-charcoal football hero, sports photography, and Persian-first layout give the site an academy-specific identity. Generic utility-page cards and unresolved dark-mode surfaces weaken that identity as visitors move between pages.

**Deterministic scan:** 7 findings across 2 rules in `src/pages`: six side-tab accent-border warnings on admin pages (`src/pages/admin/content/index.tsx:45`, `src/pages/admin/index.tsx:38, 58, 86, 114, 141`) and one color-outside-DESIGN.md advisory for `#3B82F6` in `src/pages/admin/programs/[id].tsx:56` (also imported by `new.tsx`). No detector findings in the five public page files. Side tabs may be intentional admin accents; blue may be semantic, so neither is confirmed as a defect. Browser overlay marked low contrast on dark programs/about surfaces and the shop filter panel, and a skipped heading level on Programs.

**Visual overlays:** Overlay injection succeeded in the headless browser on 10 captures (five public routes in both themes). No Human tab was available. Overlay observations are not a full WCAG audit.

## Overall Impression

The homepage’s sports-led identity survives both themes, but interior pages do not behave like one coherent theme system. The biggest opportunity is to make surface, text, and feedback colors semantic and consistent across every route, then retain only deliberate light editorial panels.

## What's Working

- The red sports hero stays legible and cohesive in light and dark modes.
- Theme preference supports light, dark, and system modes, persists, and tracks OS changes.
- The login form maintains a readable, structured dark-mode layout with labels and recovery/contact paths.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Pale text on white About surfaces in dark mode.** Dark-mode selectors change headings/body text to pale colors while `.card-glass` remains white; measured contrast was about 1.1:1 for headings and 1.6:1 for body text. This makes academy information difficult to read. Make the card surface and its text resolve to the same dark-theme tokens, or preserve a light card with dark text. Evidence: `src/styles/globals.css:319–320, 739–750`; `src/pages/about.tsx:203–208, 220–227`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`.
2. **[P1] Near-white gradient sections persist in night mode.** `/about` and `/programs` use `from-gray-50 to-gray-100`; dark overrides cover flat background utilities but not the gradient stops. Inherited pale text then sits on a bright surface, undermining readability and making the theme feel broken. Add dark-aware gradient treatment or semantic surface classes. Evidence: `src/pages/about.tsx:199`; programs route; `src/styles/globals.css:752–757`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`.
3. **[P2] Shop filter panel mixes day and night surfaces.** The shop’s filter-panel header stays white while adjacent content/controls darken, creating a visibly fragmented panel. Apply one intentional surface/text/border treatment to the whole control group. Evidence: browser overlay on `/shop` dark mode. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`.
4. **[P2] Toast notifications are hard-coded for light mode.** The shared toaster in `_app.tsx` forces white backgrounds, dark text, and pale success/error fills, so action feedback clashes with dark pages. Use theme-aware notification tokens while preserving success/error semantics. Evidence: `src/pages/_app.tsx:20–45`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`.

## Cognitive Load

Low overall, with one clear checklist failure: the desktop header exposes eight primary destinations plus cart and account actions, exceeding the four-choice working-memory guideline. Theme inconsistencies add extra effort as users move between pages. Homepage grouping and hierarchy otherwise help scanning.

## Emotional Journey

The homepage opens with football energy and clear calls to action. A visitor following it into About or Programs in night mode can encounter pale text over near-white surfaces, abruptly weakening trust and making the content feel broken. Login restores a calmer, more consistent dark layout, though light notifications can interrupt that experience.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Prospective student or parent:** night-mode About content becomes difficult to read while evaluating the academy.
- **Low-vision visitor:** measured pale-on-white text contrast is very low on About; the overlay also flagged contrast on Programs and Shop.
- **Returning student or staff member:** an action toast can appear as a bright light-theme panel on a dark page.

## Minor Observations

- Programs has a skipped heading level (`h1` to `h3`), flagged by the overlay; address separately from theme styling.
- `/news` returned HTTP 200 but rendered a Next.js Runtime Error because `/api/crawler-news` returned 500. Treat this as a functional/page-availability issue, not a theme defect.
- `/student/dashboard` and `/admin` redirected to login without credentials, so protected screens were not visually assessable.
- Anonymous `/api/auth/me` 401s were expected; Unsplash requests were blocked by the test environment. No reliable user-visible overlay outside the headless tab was available.

## Questions to Consider

- Should dark mode use warm-charcoal surfaces throughout, or preserve selected paper-like cards with appropriately dark text?
- Should anonymous visitors be able to override their device theme?
- Should the next pass prioritize About/Programs readability, shop surface consistency, or theme-aware notifications?
